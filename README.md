## English

### Educational projects in artificial intelligence and technology

This repository brings together educational projects to apply what we learned about artificial intelligence, algorithms, and educational product development during the Training Seminar on Strengthening Science and Engineering Capacities of Pre-University and Higher Education Teachers for the Dominican Republic (Phase 1), organized by Zhejiang Normal University, China.

The preliminary seminar program runs from **August 28 to September 17, 2026**. These projects primarily relate to the **August 31** sessions on generative AI, intelligent educational resource design, and the production of teaching resources and digital courses.

### Academic status

This project is an academic learning exercise and remains a work in progress. It is not a final submission or a completed product. It is awaiting feedback from the instructors teaching the seminar at Zhejiang Normal University, which will guide further revisions and improvements.

### Educational purpose

Apply the knowledge gained during the seminar by designing and developing applications that address classroom needs. The work provides practice in structuring instructions, using algorithms, creating interfaces, and integrating artificial intelligence tools to develop educational products.

### Projects in this repository

#### 1. Classroom Quiet Forest

[APP-Seminario-Uno](./APP-Seminario-Uno/) is an interactive educational application project that uses the microphone to represent progress during a quiet study activity through trees and seedlings.

It provides practice in capturing audio signals, using algorithm conditions and counters, managing time, and providing visual feedback. It relates to the program through digital educational resource development, with connections to signal and detection topics. Sound measurement and game rules alone do not constitute an AI model.

See the [requirements for the first project](./APP-Seminario-Uno/REQUIREMENTS.md).

#### 2. Educational presentations and microvideos with AI

[APP-Semininario-Dos](./APP-Semininario-Dos/) is a project aimed at creating educational presentations of 3 to 5 slides, narration scripts, and microvideos featuring digital avatars and synthesized speech.

Its purpose directly connects to the August 31 sessions: applying generative AI to teaching resource design and digital content production. Reviewing and editing the materials is part of the educational process. The availability of generation features depends on the implementation and configured services.

See the [requirements for the second project](./APP-Semininario-Dos/REQUIREMENTS.md).

### Learning put into practice

- Turn an educational need into an application proposal.
- Organize algorithms, conditions, states, and interaction sequences.
- Design digital resources and accessible classroom interfaces.
- Use AI tools to support the creation of teaching materials.
- Review the quality and educational relevance of generated content.

## Prácticas del repositorio

### 1. Bosque tranquilo para el aula

[APP-Seminario-Uno](./APP-Seminario-Uno/) es una práctica de aplicación educativa interactiva que utiliza el micrófono para representar el progreso de una actividad de estudio tranquilo mediante árboles y plántulas.

Permite trabajar la captura de señales de audio, las condiciones y contadores de un algoritmo, la gestión del tiempo y la retroalimentación visual. Su relación con el programa está en el desarrollo de recursos educativos digitales, con conexiones a los temas de señales y detección. La medición de sonido y las reglas del juego no constituyen por sí mismas un modelo de IA.

Consulta los [requisitos de la primera práctica](./APP-Seminario-Uno/REQUIREMENTS.md).

### 2. Presentaciones educativas y microvideos con IA

[APP-Semininario-Dos](./APP-Semininario-Dos/) es una práctica orientada a crear presentaciones didácticas de 3 a 5 diapositivas, guiones de explicación y microvideos con avatares digitales y voz sintetizada.

Su objetivo se vincula directamente con la jornada del 31 de agosto: aplicar IA generativa al diseño de recursos docentes y a la producción de contenidos digitales. Incluye la revisión y edición de los materiales como parte del proceso educativo. La disponibilidad de las funciones de generación depende de la implementación y de los servicios configurados.

Consulta los [requisitos de la segunda práctica](./APP-Semininario-Dos/REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Aprendizajes que se ponen en práctica

- Transformar una necesidad educativa en una propuesta de aplicación.
- Organizar algoritmos, condiciones, estados y secuencias de interacción.
- Diseñar recursos digitales e interfaces accesibles para el aula.
- Utilizar herramientas de IA para apoyar la creación de materiales didácticos.
- Revisar la calidad y pertinencia pedagógica de los contenidos generados.
